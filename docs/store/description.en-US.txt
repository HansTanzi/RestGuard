RestGuard is a break reminder that actually makes you rest.

Unlike typical break reminders, RestGuard has three enforcement levels. It can gently remind you, or it can stop you outright when you're tempted to work "just five more minutes".

■ Breaks that are actually enforced
• Every 60 minutes of work, a 10-minute break is enforced (both durations are adjustable)
• Multi-monitor support: every screen is covered
• The overlay can't be closed during a break (Alt+F4 doesn't work), and you can't quit from the tray
• Postpone for 5 minutes, up to 3 times; the count resets after a full break
• Urgent mode: when something can't wait, temporarily shrink the overlay to save your work while the countdown keeps running

■ Three enforcement levels for different situations
• High enforcement: for people who are strict with themselves but always want "five more minutes". The overlay is full-screen and can't be moved, you can postpone only once, and there's no urgent mode
• Medium enforcement (default): for everyday work. Postpone up to 3 times, with urgent mode and meeting detection. You can still work, but it gets noticeably less pleasant, nudging you to take your break
• Low enforcement: for people who just want a gentle reminder. The overlay covers 80% of the screen, and you can skip a break

■ Highly customizable
Create custom profiles based on any built-in level and fine-tune each setting: number of postpones, whether you can exit or skip breaks, urgent mode, overlay coverage (10%–100%), whether the overlay can be moved, and meeting app detection. Save multiple profiles and switch between them anytime.

■ Stays out of the way of real work
• Meeting detection: when Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex or Tencent Meeting is in the foreground, or your microphone is in use, the break waits until the meeting is over. During a break you can also click "I'm in a meeting" to be reminded again in 15 minutes
• Idle detection: 5 minutes without keyboard or mouse activity counts as being away, and the work countdown pauses; if you're away as long as a break, it counts as a break

■ Never miss the end of a break, even away from your desk
• A sound plays when the break ends: pick a built-in sound or your own audio file
• Phone notifications: get notified on Android or iOS via ntfy, so you know when to come back even when you're away from the computer; on Android it can keep ringing like an alarm until you dismiss it
• Smartwatches: phone notifications sync to Apple Watch, Wear OS, Galaxy Watch, and Xiaomi, Huawei, Garmin and other watches and fitness bands

■ Small and light
Built with Rust + Tauri, RestGuard uses the system's built-in WebView and barely uses any resources while running in the background.

■ More
• Tray menu: see the time remaining, take a break early, open settings, launch at startup, switch enforcement profiles
• 13 languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, Bengali, Portuguese, German, Japanese, Italian, Korean and Indonesian
• Free and open source, dual-licensed under MIT / Apache-2.0
• Privacy first: no data collection, no accounts, no analytics or telemetry; it only goes online when phone notifications are turned on
