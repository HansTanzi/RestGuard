Why RestGuard

Most break reminders are easy to ignore: a notification pops up, you dismiss it, and "just five more minutes" turns into two hours. Long stretches at a screen are linked to eye strain, dry eyes, neck and back pain, and repetitive strain injury (RSI). Advice like the 20-20-20 rule or a short break every hour only helps if you actually take the break.

RestGuard is for people who know they should rest but keep putting it off:

You can't just click it away.

On High, the overlay covers every screen until the break is over.

It knows when not to interrupt.

Postpones, urgent mode and meeting detection keep a break from ruining a call or costing you unsaved work.

You choose how strict it is.

Pick High, Medium or Low, or build your own profile.

Breaks that fit your day

Your day already has a rhythm: lunch, an afternoon tea, the time you head home. RestGuard follows it, so breaks come when they make sense.

A real lunch break

Turn on the lunch break and RestGuard sends you away from the screen at lunchtime, for as long as you choose. Need to check something during a long lunch? Once a regular break's worth of rest is done, the computer is yours again.

Breaks of your own

Add a tea break, a walk or a stretch at the times you choose, as many as you like. A regular break due just before one waits for it, so you never rest twice in a row.

Leave on time

Tell it when your working day ends, and it won't send you on a break in the last half hour, so you can wrap up and go home. Working late? Then the break comes after all.

Built to make breaks happen

Breaks that actually happen

A 10-minute break after every 60 minutes of work, on every monitor. Alt+F4 does nothing, and you can't quit from the tray mid-break. Want to be sure you get up? It can lock your screen too.

Highly customizable

Start a custom profile from any level and tune postpones, skipping, urgent mode, coverage and dragging. Keep as many as you like. Light or dark theme, your pick.

Stays out of the way

Waits while Zoom, Teams, Webex or Tencent Meeting is in use, and pauses the countdown when you're away from the keyboard.

Never miss the end of a break

A sound plays when the break is over, and ntfy can push it to your phone and smartwatch, so you know when to come back.

Small and fast

Rust + Tauri 2 on the system WebView. Downloads are only about 3–6 MB, dozens of times smaller than Electron-based alternatives, and it barely uses any resources in the background.

Supports your language

13 UI languages, following the system by default. Switch any time from the tray menu.

Install

Package managers keep RestGuard up to date. Direct downloads are portable and don't update on their own.

WinGet

winget install HansTanzi.RestGuard

Scoop

scoop bucket add hanstanzi https://github.com/HansTanzi/scoop-bucket
scoop install hanstanzi/restguard

Direct download

A portable single exe with no installer: put it anywhere and delete it to uninstall. Requires the WebView2 runtime (preinstalled on Windows 11).

Homebrew

brew install --cask hanstanzi/tap/restguard

Direct download

Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel. Unzip and move RestGuard.app to /Applications. The app isn't notarized, so clear the quarantine flag before first launch:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/RestGuard.app

Debian / Ubuntu (APT repository)

sudo install -d /etc/apt/keyrings
curl -fsSL https://hanstanzi.github.io/apt/hanstanzi.gpg | sudo tee /etc/apt/keyrings/hanstanzi.gpg > /dev/null
echo "deb [signed-by=/etc/apt/keyrings/hanstanzi.gpg] https://hanstanzi.github.io/apt stable main" | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/hanstanzi.list
sudo apt update && sudo apt install rest-guard

Direct download

sudo dnf install ./RestGuard_*_x86_64.rpm            # Fedora
sudo zypper install ./RestGuard_*_x86_64.rpm         # openSUSE
sudo pacman -U ./RestGuard_*_x86_64.pkg.tar.zst      # Arch

x86_64 only. The overlay needs X11. Homebrew on Linux is supported too, see the README.

Verify your download

Release files are built by GitHub Actions with a signed build provenance attestation. Check one with the GitHub CLI:

gh attestation verify RestGuard_<version>_x64.exe --repo HansTanzi/RestGuard

Or compare against SHA256SUMS.txt from the Release:

sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing          # Linux
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing      # macOS
Get-FileHash .\RestGuard_*_x64.exe                    # Windows

FAQ

Can I quit RestGuard?

Between breaks, yes: choose "Quit" in the tray menu. During a break, or while a postponed break is still owed, Quit is disabled.

What if a break comes up during a meeting?

On Medium and Low, the break waits while Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex or Tencent Meeting is in the foreground (Windows, macOS; on Windows also while it uses the microphone). If a break has already started, click "I'm in a meeting" to be reminded in an hour. On High, breaks don't wait.

I'm in the middle of something urgent. Will I lose my work?

Postpone the break by 5 minutes (up to 3 times on Medium and Low, once on High), or on Medium and Low use urgent mode to shrink the overlay and save your work while the countdown keeps running.

Does time away from the computer count as work?

No. After 5 minutes without keyboard or mouse input the work countdown pauses, and being away as long as a break counts as a full break.

macOS says the app can't be opened.

RestGuard isn't notarized by Apple. Install it with Homebrew, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/RestGuard.app once.

Does it work on Wayland?

Not reliably: Wayland doesn't let apps keep their windows on top, so use an X11 session.

Does it collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or telemetry. See the privacy policy.
